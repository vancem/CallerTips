## Target Audience

This document is for people who are just starting out (or just thinking about)
square dance calling.   You may have taken a short ‘course’ on calling, or maybe
you have been mentored by a more experienced caller, or maybe you are just
thinking about being a square dance caller.   

There are plenty of square dance callers who are more experienced than I (I have
only been calling for several years), or have been teaching calling (I have
never done that), but I have one advantage over all of them:  **I still remember
the things I had to learn ‘the hard way’**.   I also have learned to call relatively
recently, so I learned AFTER things like
[Taminations](https://www.tamtwirlers.org/taminations/),
[SqView](https://sqview.se/download.php), [music for
callers](http://musicforcallers.com/), or the [callerlab teaching
site](http://teaching.callerlab.org) existed, and thus have a ‘new caller’
perspective on how to use them to best effect.   This document is NOT meant to
be exhaustive, but hopefully you will find nuggets of information that you find
useful.   

This document got longer than I expected when I started.  Please don’t be
intimidated by its length.  It is longer partially because I wanted to SLOW DOWN
and really try to explain things in a comprehensible way.    Don’t feel like you
have to read it end-to-end.  Think of it as a menu from which you can pick and
choose and only read what catches your interest.   

To determine what to read, the table of contents is helpful.  Clicking on a
section in the table of contents brings up a link that will take you to that
topic, so you can skip around with ease.
